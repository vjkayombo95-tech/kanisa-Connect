# Operations Checklist

## Daily

Priest:

- Review today's ministry dashboard.
- Review prayer requests.
- Review Mass intentions.
- Check today's calendar and readings.

Church Admin:

- Review dashboard priorities.
- Review new members and invitations.
- Publish or update announcements.
- Check events and calendar changes.

Finance:

- Review contributions.
- Check pledges and receipts.
- Review pending reconciliation items.

Super Admin:

- Review platform dashboard.
- Check platform health and system jobs.
- Review unresolved alerts.
- Monitor support issues.

## Weekly

Priest:

- Review pastoral request completion.
- Review upcoming celebrations and ministry schedule.

Church Admin:

- Review member and invitation status.
- Review event attendance and announcements.
- Confirm calendar accuracy.

Finance:

- Review contribution trends.
- Export or review weekly reports.
- Follow up on outstanding pledges.

Super Admin:

- Review church onboarding status.
- Review logs and failed jobs.
- Review storage and backup status.
- Gather pilot feedback.

## Monthly

Priest:

- Review pastoral workload and parish engagement.

Church Admin:

- Review reports, communities, ministries, and settings.
- Confirm active roles are accurate.

Finance:

- Prepare monthly reports.
- Review reconciliation and receipts.
- Check pledge completion.

Super Admin:

- Review platform readiness.
- Review security, performance, backups, and release notes.
- Plan pilot fixes and next release scope.
